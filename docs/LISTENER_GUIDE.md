# Stellar Music Protocol — Listener & Fan Patronage Guide

## 1. The Fan-to-Artist Revolution

On traditional streaming services, less than \$0.003 of your monthly fee reaches the artist per stream. 

On **Stellar Music**, when you purchase a Music Pass for a track (e.g. 5 XLM / ~$0.50), **over 95% of the payment goes directly and instantly to the verified creator and their collaborators**.

---

## 2. Getting Started in 3 Steps

### Step 1: Connect or Generate a Wallet
- **Freighter Wallet**: Install the [Freighter browser extension](https://www.freighter.app).
- **Instant Testnet Account**: Click "Connect Wallet" -> "Instant Test Account" in the app header to automatically generate a pre-funded ed25519 keypair loaded with 10,000 testnet XLM.

### Step 2: Browse Catalog & Preview Tracks
- Browse curated tracks across Synthwave, Afrobeats, Lo-Fi, and Ambient.
- Listen to previews with the integrated mini player.

### Step 3: Purchase Music Pass
- Click "Get Pass". Confirm the transaction in Freighter.
- Upon on-chain confirmation (typically 3–5 seconds), your address is recorded on the Stellar blockchain as a lifetime pass holder.
- Lossless master audio unlocks immediately.

## 3. Playback Controls & Keyboard Shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
| :---: | :--- |
| `Space` | Toggle Play / Pause |
| `Arrow Left` | Seek backward by 5 seconds |
| `Arrow Right` | Seek forward by 5 seconds |
| `Arrow Up` | Increase volume (+5%) |
| `Arrow Down` | Decrease volume (-5%) |
| `M` | Toggle Mute |
| `Shift + /` (`?`) | Open Keyboard Shortcuts Cheatsheet |
| `0.75x - 1.5x` | Dynamic Playback Speed Adjustment |
